# Local TV

Local channels sit on **6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13 and 21–30**, like the VHF dial.

Set your ZIP in **MENU › Location** (default **04240**, Lewiston–Auburn, Maine). The weather channel follows that ZIP.

## Making a station play live
Broadcasters don't let other sites embed their live TV. Most do post newscasts, and often live streams, on **YouTube**. In **MENU › Location**, paste the station's YouTube channel link (`youtube.com/channel/UC…`) into its row. The channel then plays inside the TV. Until then it shows a card with a *Watch live* button that opens the station's site.

Open slots (22, 27–30) can be filled the same way.
