# Parental Controls

**MENU › Parental**

- **Adult channels are always locked**, PIN or not: the *After Dark* and *Half Baked* categories, 419 (Half Baked) and 957 (Lingerie Football). Only the PIN opens them.
- **18+ check:** before a PIN can be set, someone presses **I am 18 or older** and confirms. That's remembered on this device only (it never syncs to other devices).
- **PIN:** a 4-digit PIN. It unlocks adult channels and anything else you lock.
- **Locked categories:** tick any others: Horror, Comedy, Talk… The adult ones are ticked and can't be unticked.
- **Locked channels:** by default 34 (Holy Moly), 273–274 (High Times, Grow Room), 289–290 (Jackass, Wildboyz), 419 (Half Baked), 846 (Beavis and Butt-Head) and 957 (Lingerie Football). Edit the list freely; these extra locks apply once a PIN exists.
- **Kids profile:** shows only kids, cartoons, learning, nature, science, seasonal and cozy channels, plus weather, the guide and games. Everything else disappears from surfing and the guide. Turning it off needs the PIN.

A locked channel shows a 🔒 card. Type the PIN with the remote's number buttons; it stays unlocked until the TV is turned off. Changing any parental setting asks for the PIN.

Everything here (PIN, 18+ check, locks) is stored only on this device. There are no accounts.
