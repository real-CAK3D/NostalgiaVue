# NostalgiaVue Wiki

NostalgiaVue is a 1980s–2000s TV in your browser: https://nostalgiavue.vercel.app

- **[Remote & Controls](Remote-and-Controls)**: buttons, keyboard and touch
- **[Channel Lineup](Channel-Lineup)**: all 300+ channels and the dial plan
- **[Adding Channels](Adding-Channels)**: YouTube, archive.org searches, streams, M3U, shortcuts
- **[Local TV](Local-TV)**: your ZIP, local stations and the weather channel
- **[Local Media & the Bridge](Local-Media-and-the-Bridge)**: E: drive folders and Jellyfin over Tailscale
- **[Game Channel](Game-Channel)**: ROMs on channel 3
- **[Install on Mobile](Install-on-Mobile)**: the app on your phone
